KASHHT Customer Privacy Policy

User privacy, digital safety, and consumer data transparency.

Effective Year: 2026 • Entity: KASHHT Platform Operations (also registered as KASSHT) • Customer Pledge: "We value our customers"

1. Introduction & User Consent

KASHHT (hereinafter referred to as the "Company", "we", "our", or "us", operating also under the registered name KASSHT) is fully committed to safeguarding the privacy and digital security of our customers.

By accessing, browsing, or utilizing our website, platform, application, or affiliated services (collectively, the "Services"), you acknowledge and consent to the collection, receipt, storage, handling, transfer, and processing of your personal and non-personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Use. If you do not agree with any practices described herein, please discontinue using our Services.

2. Scope & Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies strictly to data gathered by KASHHT directly through online interactions, platform sessions, communication channels (SMS, electronic mail, notifications), customer support interactions, and automated telemetry.

This policy does not govern, nor do we assume responsibility for, any independent third-party websites, merchant partners, or external services that maintain separate privacy policies.

3. Information We Collect

In alignment with international consumer privacy frameworks and digital commerce standards, we classify collected data into distinct categories:

A. Personal Information

Data that identifies or can be reasonably used to identify you directly:

  • Account & Profile Details: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, physical delivery addresses, gender, and login credentials.
  • Transaction & Billing Data: Details of orders placed, payment confirmation identifiers (processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways; KASHHT never stores raw credit/debit card numbers or CVV codes), invoice receipts, and purchase history.
  • Customer Support Records: Records of queries, chat transcripts, voice support recordings, and feedback submitted to our service desk.
  • Social Media & SSO Information: Public profile information, email address, and authentication tokens if you choose to sign in using Google, Apple, or social account providers.

B. Non-Personal & Automatically Collected Data

  • Device & Hardware Telemetry: Internet Protocol (IP) address, operating system, browser type and version, device manufacturer, unique device identifiers (UDID), cellular network provider, and hardware specifications.
  • Location Data: Approximate or precise geolocation data (with your device permission) to facilitate accurate service availability, delivery estimates, and address auto-detection.
  • Usage & Navigation Data: Pages visited, interaction timestamps, search queries, referral URLs, session durations, and crash reports.

C. Device Permissions

  • SMS Permission: Used exclusively to auto-read and autofill One-Time Passwords (OTPs) during login and payment authentication to eliminate manual entry friction.
  • Phone / Dialer: Enables direct, one-tap calling to customer care and delivery executives without revealing your personal private number (using masked proxy dialing).
  • Camera / Storage: Used only when you choose to upload product photos, profile pictures, or invoices.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data for legitimate operational and business purposes, specifically:

  • To register your account, verify your identity, and manage your user profile.
  • To process orders, dispatch items, coordinate delivery logistics, and provide real-time status updates via SMS, push notifications, or email.
  • To personalize your catalog browsing experience, optimize search algorithms, and recommend relevant offerings.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions, unauthorized account access, cyber threats, and abuse of promotional offers.
  • To comply with statutory legal requirements, financial auditing rules, and official directives.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We Value Our Customers: KASHHT does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or commercial marketers. We share data only under strictly regulated conditions:

  • Delivery & Logistics Partners: Necessary delivery details (name, delivery address, masked contact number) are shared with delivery partners solely to complete fulfillment.
  • Merchants & Sellers: Basic order details to process inventory and pack items.
  • Service Providers & Cloud Infrastructure: Trusted technical partners providing cloud hosting, database encryption, SMS gateway dispatch, and payment processing under strict non-disclosure obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: Disclosures made in good faith to courts, regulatory authorities, or law enforcement bodies when mandated by applicable law.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use session cookies, persistent cookies, and web beacons to remember your authentication status, maintain shopping carts, understand feature usage, and enhance user experience. You can manage or disable cookie preferences directly through your browser settings, though certain core features may not function optimally without essential cookies.

7. Data Security, Safeguards & Retention

We employ robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures including end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit and AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest. Access to personal data is restricted strictly to authorized employees and contractors who have a legitimate business need.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required to comply with applicable tax, legal, accounting, and anti-fraud reporting mandates.

8. User Rights & Choices

In accordance with applicable data protection laws, you possess the following rights:

  • Access & Review: You may review and update your profile details directly from your account settings.
  • Correction: Request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Account Deletion / Erasure: You may request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting our privacy desk.
  • Marketing Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from non-essential promotional SMS or emails at any time using the opt-out link in the communication.
